( 06 ) FAQ

Questions,
answered

How much does a website cost?

+

A Launch Site starts from ₹14,999, a Business Website from ₹34,999, and custom web apps or AI automation are quoted per project after a free call. You get a fixed price before any work starts, so there are no surprise costs.

How long does it take to build a website?

+

A small business website is usually live in 7–10 days. A larger business website with a blog or booking takes 2–3 weeks. Web apps and AI projects are scoped individually, with weekly preview links while we build.

Will my website show up on Google?

+

Every site is built SEO-ready: fast loading, mobile-first, clean headings, meta tags, structured data, a sitemap and Google Business setup. Rankings also depend on your competition and content, so ongoing SEO and content support is available after launch.

Do you work with clients outside India?

+

Yes. Turning Wave works remotely with businesses and startups worldwide, over WhatsApp, email and video calls. Clients outside India can ask for a quote in USD.

Will I own my website and code?

+

Yes. Once the final payment is made, the website, code, content and domain are fully yours. Nothing is locked to us.

What happens after my website goes live?

+

You get help with fixes, updates and new features after launch. The Business Website package includes 30 days of free support, and ongoing support is available for every package.
