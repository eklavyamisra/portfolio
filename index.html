( FAQ ) Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does it take to build a website?

A small business website is usually live in 7–10 days. Larger websites with a blog, booking or many pages take 2–3 weeks. Web apps and AI projects are scoped individually, with weekly preview links while we build.

How do you price a website?

Every project gets a fixed quote based on the pages and features you need. Tell us about your business and you get a written plan, timeline and price within 24 hours, before any work starts.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built SEO-ready: fast loading, mobile-first, clean headings, meta tags, structured data, a sitemap and Google Business Profile setup. Rankings also depend on competition and content, so ongoing SEO support is available after launch.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. Turning Wave works remotely with businesses and startups worldwide over WhatsApp, email and video calls.

Will I own my website and code?

Yes. Once the project is complete, the website, code, content and domain are fully yours. Nothing is locked to us.

What happens after my website goes live?

You get help with fixes, updates and new features after launch, and ongoing SEO and maintenance support whenever you need it.
