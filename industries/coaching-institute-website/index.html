( FAQ ) Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does a coaching institute website take?

A single-course or tutor website is usually live in 7–10 days. Multi-course institutes with results, faculty and blog sections take 2–3 weeks. Student portals or online tests are scoped separately.

Can enquiries go directly to our counsellors?

Yes. Enquiries can be sent to email, WhatsApp or a shared Google Sheet, and we can automate follow-up messages.

Can you build landing pages for admission ads?

Yes. We build fast landing pages for Google and Meta ads with conversion tracking set up.
