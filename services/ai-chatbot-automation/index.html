( FAQ ) Good to know

Questions, answered

What can an AI chatbot do for my business?

It can answer common questions, share prices and timings, qualify leads, book appointments and hand complex conversations to your team, on your website and WhatsApp, at any hour.

Will the chatbot give wrong answers?

We train the chatbot only on your approved information, set clear limits on what it can say, and route anything uncertain to a human. You review and approve it before launch.

What kind of work can you automate?

Lead capture into sheets or CRMs, follow-up messages, invoice and report generation, data entry between tools, notifications and other repetitive tasks your team does by hand.

Do I need technical knowledge to use it?

No. We set everything up, train your team, and give you simple ways to update the chatbot's knowledge.

Can it work with my existing tools?

Usually, yes. We connect with Google Sheets, Gmail, WhatsApp Business, CRMs, payment gateways and most tools that offer an API.
