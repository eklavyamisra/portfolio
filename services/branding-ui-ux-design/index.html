( FAQ ) Good to know

Questions, answered

What do I get with brand identity design?

A primary logo and variations, a colour palette, typography, icon or mark files in every format you need (SVG, PNG, favicon), and simple guidelines to keep everything consistent.

Do you design in Figma?

Yes. All UI/UX work is done in Figma, and you get access to the files, so your team or future developers can build from them.

Can you redesign my existing logo?

Yes. We can refresh an existing logo while keeping its recognisable parts, or create a new identity from scratch.

How many revisions are included?

Each design stage includes revision rounds, agreed in your quote. We share directions early so the final design never feels like a surprise.
